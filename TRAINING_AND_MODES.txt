IMPORTANT FIXES IN THIS BUILD
- START_TRAINING.bat creates/checks .venv and installs requirements before starting.
- Historical trainer downloads 1m/5m/15m/1H public Bitget futures candles.
- 1m history is used to train LONG and SHORT logistic models and saves historical_models.json.
- Live bot loads the matching symbol/side historical model and blends it into adaptive probability.
- Emergency hedge-mode close uses SELL/CLOSE for LONG and BUY/CLOSE for SHORT.
